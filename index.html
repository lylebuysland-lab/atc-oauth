AwesomeTrumpClips

AwesomeTrumpClips is a private automation app operated solely by Lyle Rollins. It is not a public consumer product and is not offered for third-party signup.

Purpose of this app

This app connects Google Drive to a self-hosted n8n workflow so finished TikTok video clips can be uploaded to the operator’s own Drive folder after automated editing.

Who can use it

Only the developer/operator (lyle.rollins@gmail.com). There is no public login wall for this information page — this page is publicly readable.
