Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

AwesomeTrumpClips is a private n8n automation operated solely by Lyle Rollins (lyle.rollins@gmail.com).

What data we access

When you connect Google Drive, the app may create and upload video files to Google Drive folders you choose. It does not read your unrelated Drive files beyond what is required to upload and organize those clips.

How data is used

Drive access is used only to store finished video clips for the operator’s own TikTok publishing workflow. Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising.

Storage

OAuth tokens are stored in the operator’s self-hosted n8n instance. Video files are stored in Google Drive under the operator’s account.
