Privacy Policy for AwesomeTrumpClips

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the private application AwesomeTrumpClips (“the App”), operated by Lyle Rollins (“we”, “us”), handles information when Google OAuth is used to connect Google Drive to a self-hosted n8n automation.

1. Who this App is for

The App is a personal/private automation tool used only by the operator. It is not marketed to the general public and is not intended for multi-tenant consumer accounts.

2. Information we collect

When you authorize Google Drive access, Google may provide the App with OAuth tokens and basic account identifiers needed to call the Google Drive API on your behalf. The App may also process video file metadata (such as file names and folder IDs) required to upload completed clips.

We do not intentionally collect payment card numbers, government IDs, or advertising profiles through this App.

3. How we use information

  • Authenticate to Google Drive via OAuth
  • Upload finished video clips generated by the operator’s automation into Drive folders the operator selects
  • Maintain workflow reliability (for example, confirming uploads succeeded)

We do not sell personal information. We do not use Drive contents for advertising or resale.

4. Data sharing

Drive content stays in the operator’s Google account. OAuth tokens are stored in the operator’s self-hosted n8n instance. Service providers involved are limited to Google (Drive/OAuth) and the operator’s hosting for n8n. We do not share data with unrelated third parties for their marketing.

5. Data storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored on the operator’s private server running n8n. Access to that server is restricted. Video files uploaded through the App are stored in Google Drive under the connected Google account.

6. Data retention

OAuth tokens are retained until the operator disconnects the Google account in n8n or revokes access in Google Account settings. Uploaded videos remain in Drive until the operator deletes them.

7. Your choices

You may revoke the App’s Google access at any time from your Google Account security settings, or by disconnecting the credential in n8n. After revocation, the App can no longer access Drive until re-authorized.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or the App: lyle.rollins@gmail.com
